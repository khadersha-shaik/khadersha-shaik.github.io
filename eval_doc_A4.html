3. JUDGES SCORING SCALE & DEFENSE INTERROGATION GUIDE Standardized Rating
Suggested Questioning Areas During Team Defense:
  • Prompt Architecture: Did the team craft specific musical parameters (BPM, instruments, vocal timbre) or use raw/generic queries?
  • Thematic Authenticity: Does the track genuinely reflect real sacrifice, problem-solving, or gratitude vs generic party/pop tropes?
  • Workflow Mastery: How well did the team refine, iterate, master, and edit the AI audio output?
90% - 100% Exceptional
Masterful prompt, profound theme & flawless audio
75% - 89% Proficient
Solid musicality, clear structure & strong defense
60% - 74% Competent
Basic theme alignment, standard pop formula
< 60% Needs Work
Off-topic theme, minimal prompt or weak defense
